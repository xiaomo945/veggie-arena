Frequently asked

Tap a question to open it.

The game is stuck on the loading screen. What do I do?

This is the most common issue and it is almost always the browser, not the game. Work through these in order:

  • Force-refresh. Close the tab entirely and reopen the link. On iOS, also swipe the game away in the app switcher.
  • Get out of the in-app browser. If you opened the link from Instagram, Facebook, TikTok or WeChat, tap the ••• menu and choose Open in Browser. In-app browsers block the web tech this game runs on.
  • Use Chrome (Android) or Safari (iPhone). Those two are what we test against.
  • Clear your browser cache. Chrome: Menu → History → Clear browsing data. Safari: Settings → Safari → Clear History and Website Data. A half-downloaded old build sitting in cache will hang the load every time.
  • Check the connection. The game downloads its data on first load, so a weak signal can stall it at 90%.

Still stuck after all that? Email us — see the contact section below. Tell us your device model and browser version and we will dig in.

Why doesn't the game shoot when I tap? Is auto-fire broken?

It is working exactly as designed. TURNIP TROUBLE has no fire button — your weapons auto-fire continuously. There is nothing to tap to attack.

Your only input is movement: drag your thumb anywhere on screen and the turnip follows it. So instead of aiming, you are really deciding where to stand. Lead the wave, keep your distance, and let your kitchenware do the chopping.

The one exception is WOK TOSS, which only becomes available once the Wok Heat gauge fills up.

How do I unlock more weapons?

Inside a run, from the shop between waves. After every wave you clear, the shop opens and offers randomized items — new weapons, stat boosts and upgrades.

  • Weapons are bought with currency earned during that run, so they are per-run, not permanent unlocks.
  • The shop is random each time, so no two runs give you the same build. That is the roguelite part.
  • If you have never seen a certain weapon, just play more waves: the shop pool rolls differently every run.

Tip: stacking several copies of the same early weapon usually beats spreading thin across five different ones.

Is my progress saved? What happens if I clear my cache?

Progress is saved locally on your device — your high scores, best wave reached and settings. Nothing is uploaded to a server, so nothing can be synced between your phone and your computer.

The trade-off: clearing your browser cache or site data wipes that progress permanently, and we cannot restore it, because we never had a copy. Playing in private/incognito mode also means your progress disappears when you close the tab.

Same story if you remove the home-screen shortcut. Full details are in our Privacy Policy.

Which devices and browsers are supported?

TURNIP TROUBLE is built for phones in portrait orientation:

  • iPhone / iPad — Safari (iOS 15 or newer recommended).
  • Android phones — Chrome (recent versions).
  • Desktop — plays fine in Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari, using the mouse to move and the keyboard if you prefer.

It is designed one-handed and vertical, so holding your phone upright gives you the best experience. If it feels cramped, you likely opened it inside an in-app browser — escape to Chrome or Safari first.

The game drains my battery and my phone gets warm. Is that normal?

Yes, unfortunately fairly normal for a game rendering constantly at 60fps. A few things that genuinely help:

  • Drop your screen brightness — usually the single biggest battery saving.
  • Close background apps so the game is not fighting for CPU.
  • Turn off Low Power Mode frustration — no, seriously: if Low Power Mode is on, the phone throttles and the game may stutter instead.
  • Take breaks. Runs are about twenty waves, which is a natural stopping point.

Some warmth is expected, especially on older phones. If your device gets hot enough to be uncomfortable, stop playing and let it cool — that is not worth pushing through.

Audio is not playing / there is no sound.

Browsers block sound until you interact with the page, so tap the screen once after loading. Also check:

  • Your phone is not on silent — on iPhone, the physical ringer switch mutes web audio too.
  • The media volume slider is up (volume buttons while the game is running).
  • You do not have Bluetooth audio routed to a device that is out of range.

The game is fully playable muted anyway; nothing in the gameplay depends on hearing a cue.

Does TURNIP TROUBLE cost anything? Are there ads?

It is free to play in your browser, with no purchase required and no account. There are currently no ads and no analytics running in it.

If monetisation is ever added, our Privacy Policy is where it will be disclosed first.

Can I play without an internet connection?

The game needs a connection to load, since it runs in a browser rather than as an installed app. Once it is fully loaded, a short drop in signal usually will not interrupt your run — but closing the tab while offline means it cannot load again until you are back online.

Adding it to your home screen makes launching faster, but it still fetches from the web.
