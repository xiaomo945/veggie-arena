Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

Short version: TURNIP TROUBLE is a single-player casual game. We do not collect any personal information. There is no account, no login, and nothing about you leaves your device.

1. Information We Collect

We collect no personal information. None. That is not marketing language — it is how the game is built.

TURNIP TROUBLE is a single-player, offline-friendly browser game. Nothing in the gameplay needs to know who you are:

  • No account, no sign-up, no login. You never create a profile, never pick a username, never give us an email address to play.
  • No contacts. We never read or upload your address book.
  • No photos or media. We never access your photo library, camera, or microphone.
  • No location. We never ask for, read, or store GPS or any location data.
  • No tracking for advertising. We do not build an advertising profile about you.

The only thing that could loosely be called "data" is your saved progress, and that stays on your own device. See the next section.

2. Game Data Stored Locally

Your run history, unlocked records, high scores, settings and preferences are stored locally on your device — typically in your browser's local storage.

  • This data lives on your phone or computer, not on our servers.
  • We cannot read it, and nobody else can either (outside of someone with access to your unlocked device).
  • Clearing your browser cache or site data deletes this progress permanently, and we cannot restore it for you — we simply never had a copy.
  • Uninstalling the home-screen shortcut or using private/incognito mode may also reset local progress.

If you want your local data gone, clearing your browser's site data for the game is enough. There is nothing left over anywhere else to erase.

3. Third-Party Services

The game currently uses no advertising networks, no analytics SDKs, and no third-party tracking services. No ad IDs, no session recording, no crash-reporting vendor.

If that ever changes, this section will be the first place it is documented. We will list here exactly which service is being added, what data it touches, and why — and we will update the date at the top of this page before the change takes effect.

The one unavoidable party is whichever browser and app store / platform you use to reach the game. Your browser may keep its own history, cache, or permissions according to its own settings and its own privacy policy, which we do not control.

4. Children's Privacy

TURNIP TROUBLE is a casual arcade game suitable for a general audience. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Because we collect no personal information from anyone at all, there is no age-gated data collection happening behind the scenes — consistent with the requirements of COPPA (United States) and the "kids-safe" expectations of GDPR-K in the EU:

  • No personal data is knowingly collected from children under 13.
  • No behavioral advertising or profiling is used, for any age group.
  • If you believe a child has somehow transmitted personal information to us, contact us and we will look into it and delete anything we find.

Parents and guardians: since there is no chat, no user-generated content, and no external links inside gameplay, the game itself contains no social features.

5. Data Security

The most effective privacy protection here is that there is very little data to protect. Your save file never travels over the network, so it cannot be intercepted in transit.

We still follow reasonable practices on our side: the site is served over HTTPS, the game page requests only the browser permissions it actually needs (in practice, none), and we keep dependencies minimal to reduce risk.

You can help too: keep your device locked with a passcode, keep your browser updated, and avoid playing on shared devices if you care about your local high scores.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the game grows — for example if we add optional analytics or a new platform build. When we do, we will change the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

If a change is significant, we will also say so clearly on the game's main page. Continuing to play after an update means you accept the revised policy. Our direction of travel does not change though: collecting the minimum possible is the whole point.

7. Contact Us

Questions about this policy, or anything about your data? We would rather hear it and fix it than leave you guessing.

Head over to the Support & Contact page — it also has a FAQ that answers most common questions, including what to try if the game gets stuck on the loading screen.

Email: support@turniptrouble.example

TL;DR — no personal information collected, no account, no contacts, no photos, no location, no ads, no analytics. Your progress lives on your device and dies with your cache. That's the whole policy.
