How to Play

There is no aiming. There is no shooting button. Your job is feet and timing.

  1. Drag to move

    Thumb anywhere on the screen and your turnip follows it. That's the entire movement system — so simple you'll be playing properly in about four seconds.

  2. You auto-fire

    Your weapons never stop shooting. All you control is where you stand. Positioning is the skill: lead the wave, don't chase it.

  3. Dash with the DASH button

    The DASH button sits bottom-right. Tap it to dart away — you're invincible for the length of the dash, so use it to punch out of a closing circle. It then takes 1.8 seconds to recharge, so don't waste it on nothing.

  4. Walk over the coins

    Cooked enemies drop coins where they fall. Get within magnet range and they fly to you; miss a few and they're swept up when the wave ends. Either way, they go to the shop.

  5. Build Wok Heat

    Every enemy you cook fills the heat gauge. Keep it climbing — bodies, pressure, panic, that's all normal. Don't let the gauge sit cold.

  6. Hit the Wok Toss

    When Wok Heat is full, the WOK TOSS button lights up. Tap it for a screen-wide slam that clears space and buys you breathing room.

  7. Shop between waves

    Spend your haul on weapons and upgrades, then straight into the next wave. Twenty of those and the run is yours.

Quick questions

Is TURNIP TROUBLE really free?

Yes. Play the full game in your browser right now, no payment and no account. Nothing to download.

Do I need to install anything?

Nope. It runs in the browser on your phone. You can optionally add it to your home screen so it launches like an app, but that's just a shortcut.

Is it like Brotato?

If you love Brotato's auto-firing arena loop, this will feel familiar — auto-fire, shop between waves, stack upgrades. The difference: portrait one-handed play plus the Wok Heat / Wok Toss mechanic.

How long is one run?

Twenty waves. Short enough for a coffee break, chaotic enough that you'll want another go immediately.

What is the DASH button for?

It's the escape hatch in the bottom-right corner. Tap it and you lurch forward with invincibility frames for the length of the dash — perfect for punching out of a circle that just closed around you. It recharges in 1.8 seconds, so spend it when it buys you something.

Why didn't my coins get picked up?

Coins drop where an enemy dies and only fly to you once you're inside magnet range — walk closer and they'll come to you. Anything you leave behind gets swept up automatically when the wave ends, so nothing is really lost.

How do I pick a different character?

On the title screen, tap the card of the veg you want: Turnip (balanced), Potato (tanky), Tomato (attack speed) or Chili (fast). Each one gives something up, so there's no strictly best pick.

Does it work on my old phone?

Probably — the game is lightweight and browser-based. If it loads, it runs. Keyboard controls are also available if you're on desktop.

Ready to get cooked?

Twenty waves. Twelve weapons. Four veggies. One thumb. Absolutely no reason not to try it.
