Privacy Policy

TURNIP TROUBLE / 《萝卜突围》 — mobile portrait arena survival roguelite

English

How we handle your data

TURNIP TROUBLE stores all game progress locally on your own device only. Your save data (current run, unlocked weapons, upgrades, best wave, settings) is written to the device's local storage at user:// — the standard sandboxed save path used by the game engine. It never leaves your device.

  • No personal data collected. We do not collect, transmit, or store any personally identifiable information (PII) — no name, email, phone number, location, contacts, or device identifier.
  • No third-party tracking. The game contains no analytics SDKs, no advertising SDKs, and no third-party trackers of any kind.
  • No account system. There is no login, no registration, and no user profile.
  • No server uploads. The game runs entirely on-device. Nothing is uploaded to any server.

Children's privacy

The game is designed to be suitable for a general audience and does not knowingly collect any personal information from children. In line with the principles of the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the GDPR, since we collect no personal data from any user — child or adult — there is nothing to disclose or share.

Your rights (GDPR)

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is no personal data to access, correct, export, or delete. You remain in full control of your save file, which you can clear at any time by uninstalling the game or clearing the app's local storage on your device.

Future changes

If we ever add online features such as cloud saves, leaderboards, or cross-device sync, this policy will be updated and we will request your explicit consent before any personal data is collected or transmitted. The current version will always be available at this page.

我们如何处理您的数据

《萝卜突围》的所有游戏进度仅保存在您本机设备。 存档（当前局、已解锁武器、强化、最高波次、设置）写入设备的本地存储空间 user:// —— 即游戏引擎使用的标准沙箱存档路径，永远不会离开您的设备。

  • 不收集个人身份信息。 我们不会收集、传输或存储任何可识别个人身份的信息（PII）——包括姓名、邮箱、电话、地理位置、通讯录或设备标识符。
  • 不含第三方追踪。 游戏内没有任何数据分析 SDK、广告 SDK 或任何形式的第三方追踪器。
  • 无账号系统。 没有登录、注册或用户档案。
  • 无服务器上传。 游戏完全在本地运行，任何数据都不会上传到服务器。

儿童隐私

本游戏面向大众玩家设计，不会故意收集任何儿童的个人信息。依据《儿童在线隐私保护法》（COPPA）与 GDPR 的基本原则，由于我们不向任何用户（无论儿童或成人）收集个人数据，因此不存在需要披露或共享的信息。

您的权利（GDPR）

由于我们不持有您的任何个人数据，也就不存在可供访问、更正、导出或删除的个人数据。存档完全由您掌控——您可随时通过卸载游戏或清除本应用的本地存储来删除存档。

未来变更

若未来新增联网功能（如云存档、排行榜或跨设备同步），本政策将同步更新，并在收集或传输任何个人数据前征得您的明确同意。最新版本始终可在本页面查看。
