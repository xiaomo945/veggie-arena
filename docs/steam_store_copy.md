## English

### Short Description（短描述，~80 字符，抓眼球）
**A turnip fights off the kitchen with pans, forks & microwaves. Roguelite arena survival.**

### Long Description（长描述，Brotato 风格）
**TURNIP TROUBLE** is a single-player, vertically-oriented roguelite arena survivor. You are a brave turnip trapped in a hostile kitchen — survive wave after wave of angry food, grab the loot, and build the silliest weapon combo you can.

**Wield kitchen weapons.**
Pans, microwave blasts, forks, ladles, teapots, blenders — 12 unlockable tools of chaos. Auto-fire at the nearest enemy while you focus on movement and positioning.

**Master the Wok Heat.**
Land hits and kills to build up "锅气" (Wok Heat). Climb the tiers for attack-speed and damage buffs, then unleash a screen-clearing Wok Toss when the heat is maxed.

**Build your combo.**
Between waves, spend gold at the supply station on upgrades and new weapons. Stack synergies, stack pain. Every run is a fresh experiment.

**Survive the arena.**
Endless escalating waves, boss encounters, and a tight single-screen arena that plays great on Steam Deck (portable / controller-friendly, vertical layout).

**Roguelite progression.**
Earn unlocks across runs — new weapons, higher best waves, more total kills — so there's always a reason to dive back in.

- 🥕 Pure single-player, pick-up-and-play runs
- 🍳 12 kitchen weapons, deep build variety
- 🔥 Wok Heat ultimate mechanic
- 🎮 Steam Deck verified-ready (vertical, controller support)
- 🏆 Steam Achievements & Stats

## 简体中文（国区）

### Short Description（短描述）
**萝卜用平底锅、叉子和微波炉杀出厨房！竖屏 Roguelite 竞技场生存。**

### Long Description（长描述）
**《萝卜突围》**是一款单人竖屏 Roguelite 竞技场生存游戏。你是一根勇猛的萝卜，被困在充满敌意的大厨房里——撑过一波又一波愤怒的食物，捡起战利品，凑出最离谱的武器组合。

**厨房即武器。**
平底锅、微波射线、叉子、汤勺、茶壶、搅拌机……12 把可解锁的"厨具"。自动锁定最近敌人开火，你只需专注走位。

**玩转"锅气"。**
命中与击杀积攒锅气，火候分档提升攻速与伤害；锅气拉满即可释放横扫全屏的"颠勺"终结技。

**组合 build。**
波次之间在补给站花金币买强化、换武器，叠出流派与连锁反应，每一局都是新实验。

**守住竞技场。**
不断升级的波次、Boss 来袭、紧凑的单屏竖版竞技场，在 Steam Deck 上掌机/手柄体验俱佳。

**Roguelite 进程。**
跨局解锁新武器、刷新最高波数、累计击杀，总有一个理由让你再来一局。

- 🥕 纯单人，随时开一局
- 🍳 12 把厨具，build 深度足
- 🔥 锅气终极机制
- 🎮 适配 Steam Deck（竖屏 + 手柄）
- 🏆 支持 Steam 成就与统计
