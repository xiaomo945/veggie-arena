### About This Game

**TURNIP TROUBLE is a one-handed arena survival roguelite where a turnip fights back with Chinese kitchenware.** Think Brotato meets a wok station: monsters swarm from every side, your weapons never stop firing, and your only job is to keep moving and stay alive.

**The loop is fast and repeatable.** One run is 20 waves of ~20 seconds each. You drag to steer with your left thumb while your arsenal auto-fires on the right — no aiming, no fire button, just positioning. Dash out of a closing circle with i-frames on a 1.8s cooldown, sweep up dropped coins with your magnet, then hit the between-wave shop to buy weapons and grab randomized upgrades. Build **Wok Heat** and unleash the signature **Wok Toss** to flip the entire arena and clear the screen.

**Four veggies, twelve kitchen weapons, and a roster of angry produce.** Pick Turnip (balanced), Potato (tanky, slow), Tomato (fast attack, fragile) or Chili (quick, low damage) — every bonus costs you something. Cook with Cleaver, Skewer, Ladle, Teapot, Blender and more, and survive waves of Onion minions, Charging Scallions, armored Winter-Melon with pot-lid shields, Dragonfly fliers, and a Pressure-Cooker Boss.

**It's snappy, silly, and built for five-minute sessions.** Bright cartoon-veggie art, a Chinese-kitchen sense of humor, three original BGM tracks (menu / battle / boss) and juicy SFX make every run feel like a lunch rush gone glorious. Free to try in the browser, and tuned for that "okay, one more run" itch.

**Coming to Steam with full controller and Steam Deck support.** Already playable free in your browser today; the Steam release adds gamepad handling (dash on A / RT), cloud-friendly saves and a polished big-screen experience. Wishlist now and bring the wok to your library.

### 关于这款游戏

**《萝卜突围》是一款单手操作的竞技场生存 Roguelite，主角是一颗拿中式厨具打架的萝卜。** 可以把它想成「Brotato 遇上中式灶台」：蔬菜敌人从四面八方涌来，你的武器永不停火，你要做的就是不停走位、活下去。

**节奏快、可重复、上手即爽。** 单局 20 波，每波约 20 秒。左手拖动走位，右手武器自动开火——没有瞄准、没有开火键，只有站位与时机。用 1.8 秒冷却的冲刺（带无敌帧）闪出包围圈，磁吸捡金币，波间进商店买武器、抽随机强化。攒满「锅气」放一记招牌「颠勺」，全屏翻盘清场。

**四种蔬菜、十二种厨具、一整支愤怒的食材大军。** 选萝卜（均衡）、土豆（高血低速）、番茄（高攻速脆皮）或辣椒（高速低伤）——每个加成都对应一份代价。菜刀、签子、汤勺、茶壶、搅拌机齐上阵，迎战洋葱小兵、冲刺葱、锅盖盾冬瓜重甲、青蜻蜓飞虫，以及高压锅 Boss。

**爽快、搞笑、专治碎片时间。** 明亮卡通蔬菜画风，中式厨房式幽默，三段原创 BGM（菜单/战斗/Boss）加扎实音效，每一局都像一场鸡飞狗跳的午市高峰。浏览器即开即玩，精准戳中「再来一局」的瘾。

**即将登陆 Steam，完整支持手柄与 Steam Deck。** 现已可在浏览器免费试玩；Steam 版将加入手柄操作（A / RT 冲刺）、更适合大屏的体验。立即加入愿望单，把这只灶台萝卜带回家。

### 已修正的错误
- 原文 EN/CN 在「武器举例」中把 **高压锅 / Pressure-Cooker** 列为玩家可用厨具武器之一。对照 `data/`：高压锅实为 **Boss 敌人**（`enemies.json` 的 `boss`），并非玩家武器；`weapons.json` 中并无"高压锅"武器。已将两处举例里的 Pressure-Cooker / 高压锅删除（保留 Cleaver/Skewer/Ladle/Teapot/Blender 等真实武器 + "and more"）。「12 把武器」的总数表述本身正确，无需改动。
